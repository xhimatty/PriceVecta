Visual Monitoring Dashboard

A clean dashboard that displays your tracked products and their latest prices. Price decreases are highlighted instantly, while increases are clearly flagged so you can identify market movements at a glance.

Receive notifications the moment a price change is detected. Every alert includes the new price, previous price, and direct product link so you can react quickly.

Every price change is stored automatically in your database. Export historical data anytime as CSV files for reporting, trend analysis, and business intelligence.
